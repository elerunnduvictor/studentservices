Strategy Statement

Student Services delivers an intuitive and supportive service experience that enables student progression from admission to graduation.

Student Services is responsible for providing tools, processes, and supports to complement the core academic experience. Our success is defined by achieving maximum student autonomy, satisfaction, and completion, with the ideal state being students who navigate the experience intuitively and independently—to their delight—achieving certificates and degrees within a reasonable timeframe.
