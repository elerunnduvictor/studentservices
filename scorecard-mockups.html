“How far from Green?” becomes a picture. The runway is drawn from the sheet's own cutoffs; the marker is the live value. Lower-is-better KPIs flip zones automatically. Once bi-weekly refreshes accrue, a trend sparkline slots in beneath the runway.

Built to sit beside the OKR Progress page — same navbar, fonts (Cormorant Garamond / Jost on the real site), teal-and-gold palette, light & dark themes, and login protection. Data flows from the “KPI ScoreCard” Excel tab through a refresh script, exactly like the OKR bi-weekly update. Only measures with “Tracking” ticked appear on the scorecard — the 50 “Not Tracking” rows are excluded entirely.
